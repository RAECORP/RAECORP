About RAECORP

More Than Property. We Invest In Possibilities.

RAECORP is an international property management and solutions company focused on connecting property owners and sellers with a strong database and network of qualified buyers.

We go beyond simply connecting a buyer and seller. Our approach is centred around understanding the property, understanding the circumstances and identifying a suitable path forward.

Through diligent property assessment, strategic positioning, buyer connections and transaction support, RAECORP seeks to create outcomes that work for the people behind each property.

"We don't just invest in properties. We invest in people's lives."
